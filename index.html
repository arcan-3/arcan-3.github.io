AboutÜber mich

Machine learning engineer and researcher working on the recovery of structure from noisy physical measurements: acoustic, vibration, optical and time-series. My doctorate built aMP, an end-to-end deep learning pipeline that replaces manual staging of meiosis in live-cell confocal microscopy, combining segmentation-based motion stabilisation, ensemble object localisation, constrained tracking and state-vector sequence modelling.

Earlier work was in underwater acoustics: channel estimation in a water column, chirp-modulated communication, and passive SONAR source separation. I now lead industrial AI at BATO GmbH, owning platform architecture, on-premise deployment and technical delivery to manufacturing customers.

I have worked in research and industry in parallel since 2015, including in regulated environments where correctness is defined by a standard text and an audit trail rather than by a validation metric alone. Python and PyTorch daily; C++ in production at DNV and Scalian; German at B2. Alongside this I am completing a part-time MBA in Technology Management at the Northern Institute of Technology Management (NIT), TUHH.
