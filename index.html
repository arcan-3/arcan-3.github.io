The microscopy constraint

Photons are the budget, and the subject sets it.

In live-cell confocal microscopy every photon you add improves signal-to-noise and damages the living cell. So all usable data is deliberately under-illuminated: the images you get are the ones the specimen survived.

My doctorate built the aMP Toolkit on that constraint — automated staging of meiosis in exactly this under-exposed regime. It is the same trade-off as dose reduction in medical imaging, and the same trade-off as a passive receiver that cannot ask the channel for more energy.
