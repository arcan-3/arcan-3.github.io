Getting structure back out of noisy measurements.

For twelve years I have worked on one problem in different physics: something real is happening, the sensor gives you a degraded version of it, and you have to recover enough to act on. Water columns, whale clicks, vibrating machinery, living cells under a microscope that you are not allowed to illuminate properly.

Measured Recovered
6 dB

Drag it down. The measurement falls apart long before the underlying signal does. Low-dose imaging, passive sonar and a bearing failing inside a running machine are all this picture. The recovery drawn here is a sliding quadratic fit — a deliberately trivial method, because the figure is about the problem, not about the answer.
