SummaryKurzprofil

Machine learning engineer and researcher working on the recovery of structure from noisy physical measurements: acoustic, vibration, optical and time-series. Doctorate on aMP, an end-to-end deep learning pipeline replacing manual staging of meiosis in live-cell confocal microscopy. Earlier work in underwater acoustics. Now technical lead for industrial AI at BATO GmbH, owning platform architecture, on-premise deployment and technical delivery to manufacturing customers. Python and PyTorch daily; C++ in production at DNV and Scalian; German at B2. Currently completing a part-time MBA in Technology Management at the NIT, TUHH.
